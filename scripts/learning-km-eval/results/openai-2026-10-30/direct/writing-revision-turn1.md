# Proposed Workshop: Turning Notes into Useful Drafts

## A practical approach to everyday notes

Organizers are proposing a community workshop about organizing personal notes and shaping them into clear drafts. The pilot is pending approval.

For example, a participant might sort notes from a neighborhood meeting into decisions, unanswered questions and action items. They could then write a follow-up message confirming what was agreed, asking for missing information and outlining next steps.

## How to stay informed

The workshop’s date, venue, capacity and price have not been confirmed. Sign-ups are not open and will not begin unless the pilot is approved.

If you may be interested in attending, contact the organizers to express your interest. Further information, including registration details, will be shared if approval is granted.
