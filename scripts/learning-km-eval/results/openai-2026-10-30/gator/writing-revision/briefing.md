# Proposed Workshop: Turning Notes into Clear Drafts

## A practical process

Organizers are proposing a community workshop about organizing personal notes and turning them into useful drafts. The pilot is pending approval, and its date, venue, capacity and price have not been confirmed. Sign-ups are not open.

The workshop would explore a simple path from rough notes to clear writing. For example, a participant could review notes from a neighborhood meeting, separate decisions from unanswered questions, and draft a follow-up explaining what was decided and what still needs discussion.

## Express your interest

Community members who would like updates can contact the organizers to express interest. This is the useful next step while the proposal is being considered. Further details will be shared only if and when they are confirmed.
